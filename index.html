FUTURE OF PREMIUM

The Designer

Denim’s next chapter is woven around the lives we lead- where innovation brings comfort, freedom and authenticity. Bridging heritage craftsmanship, cultural storytelling, and innovation together to push denim lifestyle forward with purpose. Timeless yet unexpected: rooted in authenticity, engineered for modern life, and designed to endure.

Nick Zipagan is a design leader whose work connects heritage craftsmanship, cultural storytelling, and innovation. Raised in New York City and educated at Parsons and FIT, his perspective draws on a career spanning couture, contemporary fashion, and American heritage- from Anne Valérie Hash in Paris to Ralph Lauren, American Eagle, Gap, and Tommy Hilfiger.

At Ralph Lauren, an immersion in vintage garments and global archives shaped his appreciation for the history held within clothing. That sensibility continues to inform his work: preserving the character of the original while finding new relevance through fit, fabric, and function.

Nick approaches design with curiosity and intention. His focus is on creating products that deepens our connection to what we wear- bringing greater comfort to everyday life and lasting value through thoughtful, responsible design.
